Abdul Quadri Busari (IBN-ISIAQ) is a Computer Science student at Osun State University, a writer, student advocate, and motivational speaker passionate about inspiring young people through words and ideas. As a writer, he shares reflections on personal growth, student life, discipline, and the realities of society. Through his writings, he seeks to challenge conventional thinking, encourage self-development, and inspire young people to become better versions of themselves. Beyond writing, he is developing his skills in web development and public speaking, driven by a desire to learn, create, and make a meaningful impact on society. He believes that words have the power to change perspectives, inspire action, and give hope to people who need it most.

My journey into web development involves learning, practising, solving problems, and building projects to improve my skills.
